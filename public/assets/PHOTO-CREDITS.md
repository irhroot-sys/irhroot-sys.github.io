The photographs below are used as licensed, representative industrial imagery. They do not depict AALKC facilities unless explicitly stated.

- `service-industrial-dismantling.webp` — Yasin Hemmati, [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/lp6CBQSr1Ek), used under the Unsplash License. Also used as the homepage hero image.
- `service-heavy-logistics.webp` — Tahir mq, [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jeddah_Islamic_Port_1.jpg), used under CC BY-SA 4.0. Jeddah Islamic Port, Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, photographed 2019. Cropped/resized from the original; no other alterations. Replaces a prior placeholder image that depicted an American desert highway and was not representative of the Kingdom.

- `service-scrap-metal.webp` — source and license undocumented. Used on the Scrap Purchasing service card and on four material cards (copper, heavy melt steel, aluminum, brass). A reverse-image and photographer-portfolio search did not surface an exact match on Pexels, Unsplash, or Wikimedia Commons; the image shows no signs of AI generation and no geographic markers that would make it inappropriate, but its license could not be positively confirmed. Recommend AALKC confirm the original purchase/download record, or commission a replacement, before this file is relied on for commercial use at scale.
- `service-construction-materials.webp` — source and license undocumented. Used on the Materials page hero and on three material cards (rebar, stainless steel, plate/structural). Same status and recommendation as above.

## Replaced

`hero-dammam-scrap-yard-v2.webp` was an AI-generated image (created with OpenAI ImageGen) previously used as the homepage hero. It was replaced everywhere in the site's code with the real, licensed photograph above (`service-industrial-dismantling.webp`). The file itself could not be deleted from the repository through the tools available for this change (no file-delete capability), so its contents have been overwritten with a real, licensed photograph (Tahir mq, Jeddah Islamic Port, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0) to ensure no AI-generated pixels remain in the repository under any filename. The file is unreferenced by any page and can be safely deleted in a future commit with `git rm public/assets/hero-dammam-scrap-yard-v2.webp`.
